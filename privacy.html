Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how EsportsPulse handles information when you use our website.

1. Introduction

EsportsPulse ("EsportsPulse", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy applies to the EsportsPulse website and explains the types of information that may be collected, how that information may be used, when it may be shared and the choices available to users.

2. Who We Are

EsportsPulse is an independent esports and gaming information platform.

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at:

Email: esportspulse.in@gmail.com

3. Information We May Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use EsportsPulse.

3.1 Information You Provide

If you contact us by email or use a contact feature provided on the website, we may receive information such as your name, email address and the contents of your message.

Please do not send passwords, payment card details, government identification numbers or other highly sensitive information through ordinary email unless specifically requested through a secure process.

3.2 Automatically Collected Information

Depending on the hosting provider, security services, analytics tools or other technologies used by the website, certain technical information may be processed automatically.

This may include information such as IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring pages, approximate location derived from an IP address, pages visited and technical logs.

The exact information collected depends on the services actually enabled on EsportsPulse.

3.3 Cookies and Similar Technologies

EsportsPulse may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for website functionality, security, preferences, analytics or other permitted purposes.

Where applicable law requires consent for certain cookies or tracking technologies, the relevant consent mechanism will be provided.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Operating and maintaining EsportsPulse.
  • Responding to user questions and support requests.
  • Improving website performance and user experience.
  • Protecting the website against abuse, fraud and security threats.
  • Understanding website usage where analytics are enabled.
  • Maintaining technical logs and website security.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.
  • Managing communications that you have requested.

5. Legal Bases for Processing

Where privacy laws such as the GDPR apply, personal information may be processed on an appropriate legal basis depending on the circumstances.

These bases may include consent, performance of a contract or requested service, compliance with a legal obligation, protection of legitimate interests, or another lawful basis permitted by applicable law.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent where applicable. Withdrawal does not necessarily affect processing that occurred before consent was withdrawn.

We may use third-party providers for services such as website hosting, security, analytics, content delivery, email, APIs and other technical functions.

Some third-party service providers may process information in countries other than the country in which you live.

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by applicable law.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information.

These may include, where applicable:
